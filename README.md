## Research Highlights

At the University of Electro-Communications in Tokyo, I completed the JUSST academic exchange program from October 2024 to September 2025. Under Prof. Keiji Yanai at the Yanai Laboratory, I worked on **BengaliDiff**, a diffusion model for few-shot Bengali font generation, and presented the research at ICDAR 2025 in Wuhan, China.

My ongoing undergraduate thesis, **Uncertainty-Guided Diffusion Network for Multi-Domain Biomedical Image Segmentation**, explores biomedical image segmentation under the supervision of Dr. Md. Fokhray Hossain.
